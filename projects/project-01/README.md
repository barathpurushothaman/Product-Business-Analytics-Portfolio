# Project 01 – Olist E-Commerce Business Analysis

> An end-to-end business analysis of the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to uncover actionable business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Project Status

🟡 In Progress

Current Phase: Dataset Understanding & Business Discovery

---

## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to understand the business, identify meaningful business problems, perform data-driven analysis, and provide actionable recommendations.

Rather than starting with predefined assumptions, this project follows a structured analytical approach where business questions emerge through exploration of the data.

---

## Dataset

**Source:** Kaggle

**Dataset:** Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset

The dataset contains anonymized information about:

- Customers
- Orders
- Products
- Sellers
- Payments
- Reviews
- Geolocation
- Product Categories
